OffChat

Chat with people nearby, without internet.

Privacy Policy Support

OffChat talks directly between phones over Bluetooth, and over Wi-Fi between iPhones when the app is open. Messages can hop through other nearby phones to reach further. There is no account to create, no phone number to share and no server in the middle.

  • Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted and every message is signed.
  • Channels for groups, with optional passwords, joined by QR code or invitation.
  • Photos, short voice messages, replies, reactions and profile photos.
  • Available in 15 languages.

Questions? See Support. To learn what OffChat does with your data (very little), read the Privacy Policy.
