Getting started

Why can’t I see anyone?

OffChat only connects to other phones that have OffChat open or running in the background, and chatting needs at least two. Check that Bluetooth is on, that you allowed Bluetooth for OffChat in Settings, and that the other person is within a few metres. Walls and crowds reduce the range. Phones that do not run OffChat are never connected to.

Does it work without internet or mobile signal?

Yes, that is the point. The only time you need a connection is to buy or restore OffChat Plus for the first time.

Can I chat with someone far away?

Messages can hop through other phones running OffChat, up to five hops, so they can travel further than one Bluetooth link. If nobody is in between, the other person has to be nearby.

Messages

My message says it was sent but not delivered.

Direct messages wait on your phone and are tried again for up to 24 hours when the other person comes within reach. Messages in channels are sent once to whoever is nearby and have no delivery confirmation.

Are my messages private?

Direct messages and messages in password-protected channels are end-to-end encrypted, and every message is signed so it cannot be forged. Messages in an open channel are not encrypted. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I join a channel?

Scan its QR code, open an invitation a friend sent you in a chat, or type its name (and password, if it has one). Joining by QR code or invitation is always free.

OffChat Plus

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in OffChat, tap OffChat Plus, then Restore Purchases. You need an internet connection once. After that Plus works offline.

What does Plus include?

HD photos, longer voice messages and texts, unlimited channels you create by name, colour themes, disappearing messages and chat export. One payment, no subscription.

I was charged but Plus is locked.

Try Restore Purchases first. If that does not help, write to me and, if you can, include the order number from your Apple receipt.

Your data

OffChat has no accounts or servers, so I cannot see or recover your chats. They live only on your phone and the phones you chatted with. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and how to delete it.
