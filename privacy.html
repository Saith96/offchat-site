Privacy Policy

OffChat · Effective 26 October 2026

Home Support
In short: OffChat has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. What you write stays on your phone and on the phones of the people you chat with.

1. Who is responsible

OffChat is made by Matteo Saitta (“I”). You can reach me at saithdevelopments@gmail.com.

2. What OffChat does with information

Stored on your phone only

OffChat keeps the following on your phone and never uploads it to me or to any server: your display name, your bio and profile photo, your messages, photos and voice messages, the people and channels you have added, your settings and your encryption keys. The keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. Because it all lives on your phone, it may be included in your own device backups if you have backups turned on.

Shared with people nearby

When your phone connects to other phones running OffChat (over Bluetooth, and over Wi-Fi between iPhones), it shares what is needed to chat:

  • Your display name and your public keys, so others can recognise you and send you encrypted messages.
  • A short fingerprint of your profile photo and bio. The photo and bio themselves are sent, encrypted, only to a person who asks for them.
  • The messages, photos and voice messages you choose to send.

Anyone nearby who uses OffChat can see that your phone is using OffChat and can see your display name. You can hide your phone from new connections in the app; people you are already connected to keep their link.

Passing on other people’s messages

To reach further than Bluetooth range, your phone passes on messages for other people, and they pass on yours. A phone that passes on a message cannot read it if it is encrypted (direct messages and password-protected channels). It can see who it is from and for, which channel, when it was sent and how big it is. Messages waiting to be handed on are kept in the phone’s memory for up to 24 hours and then discarded.

Open channels are not secret

Messages in a channel without a password are not encrypted. Anyone nearby who joins that channel, and any phone that passes the message on, can read them. Use a direct message or a password-protected channel for private conversations.

3. What is not collected

OffChat does not ask for or collect an account, phone number, email address, your contacts, your location, or an advertising identifier. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet to send me anything. If you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give me anonymous crash reports; I receive nothing otherwise.

4. Permissions

  • Bluetooth: to find nearby people who use OffChat and to send and receive messages.
  • Local network: to chat with nearby iPhones over Wi-Fi when the app is open.
  • Camera: to take photos to send, and to scan QR codes.
  • Microphone: to record voice messages.
  • Notifications: to tell you about new messages. They are created on your phone; no notification server is involved.

Each permission is optional and you can change it at any time in iOS Settings. The matching feature stops working if you turn it off.

5. Purchases

OffChat Plus is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. I do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple may give me anonymous sales totals.

6. Your choices

  • Delete a message, or leave a channel (which also removes its messages), on your phone at any time. With OffChat Plus, a chat can delete its messages automatically after an hour, a day or a week.
  • Deleting the app removes your messages and settings. iOS can keep items in the Keychain after an app is deleted, so your identity key may remain and be used again if you reinstall OffChat.
  • A message you already sent exists on the phones that received it. I cannot remove it from them, and they control their own copy.

Because I do not hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete on my side. If you have a question about your rights, write to me.

7. Children

OffChat is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. There are no accounts. Note that nearby people can message anyone who is visible on OffChat.
